Doubloon: Coin Merge Puzzle

Slide, merge and double your coins. Doubloon is a relaxing number-merging puzzle where every coin you combine grows your fortune. Start with the Japanese Yen, build your wealth and unlock six more currencies, each with its own colors, music and a rule that changes how you play.

  • Free to play
  • 7 currencies
  • 11 languages
  • Google Play Games leaderboards

More ways to play

  • 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5 boards
  • Time Attack: two minutes on the clock
  • Daily Puzzle: the same board for everyone
  • Challenge a friend with a board code
  • Daily quests, login streaks and weekly events
  • 24 achievements and detailed statistics
  • Undo, Hammer, Swap and Shuffle jokers
  • Tile skins to restyle your coins

About

A studio of one, from first sketch to store listing.

I'm Deniz Can Oğuz, a developer based in Türkiye. Under the DCO Studio name I handle everything a game needs: the rules, the code, the visuals, the translations and the release.

01

Native, no engine

Games are written in Kotlin and drawn directly on the Android canvas, with no game engine in between.

02

Built for everyone

Doubloon ships in 11 languages, including right-to-left Arabic, so players can enjoy it in their own.

03

Respectful by default

No sign-up and no servers of my own. Your progress stays on your device, and ad consent is asked where the law requires it.
